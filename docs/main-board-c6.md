# Main board with ESP32-C6: module, pinout, motor driver, layer stack, routing

Status: 2026-11-11 · Describes the current state of the main board (board A, `hardware/main-board/`): **rev B**
(buck converter U3, branch `revb-buck`), the design to order; rev A with the 3.3 V LDO stays on `main` as a fallback.
Current decisions: **ESP32-C6-MINI-1**, motor driver **DRV8213DSG**, **USB-C J4** on the board (flashing
only via J4), **buck converter TPS62162** for 3.3 V, **4 layers**, machine-assembled on one side only (bottom side only pads, test points and DNP footprints).
Rejected variants (C3, DRV8833, 2 layers, earlier USB variants, 3.3 V LDO): [decision-history.md](decision-history.md).
Background: [analysis-wireless-controllers.md](analysis-wireless-controllers.md) 3b, [hardware-concept.md](hardware-concept.md),
[idea-usb-c-main-board.md](idea-usb-c-main-board.md). Firmware: [firmware-architecture.md](firmware-architecture.md).

Markers: **(verified)** = source read or recalculated against the project files · **(uncertain)** = secondary source,
estimate, or not checked in the datasheet.

## Summary

- **Module:** ESP32-C6-MINI-1, target **-H8 (8 MB)**, fallback **-N4 (4 MB)**. Both are pin- and footprint-compatible,
  so the choice is made only when ordering. Since 2026-11-06 the H8 is also in stock at JLC (620 pcs).
- **Footprint:** custom `lcsc:WIFIM-SMD_ESP32-C6-MINI-1`. Do **not** use the KiCad footprint `RF_Module:ESP32-C6-MINI-1`:
  its courtyard contains a 43 × 20 mm antenna keepout zone (breaks `antenna_keepout()`), and a
  3D model is missing.
- **Motor driver:** **DRV8213DSG** (WSON-8 2 × 2 mm) with current sensing (IPROPI), current limiting (2 A) and autosleep.
  No shunt, no nSLEEP/nFAULT, only 2 GPIOs.
- **Lever:** "lever closed" = HIGH (R3 to GND, R4 to 3V3); the lever wakes the chip with ANY_HIGH on IO3.
- **USB-C J4** with ESD (flow-through), CC resistors and power path (D2 for VBUS, Q1 for BAT_IN).
- **Supply:** VSYS 3.0–6.6 V: 1S Li-ion, USB, 3 × or 4 × AA; buck converter **TPS62162** (U3) with 2.2 µH (L1):
  approx. 50 mW loss at 6.4 V, board standby approx. 25 µA, cut-off at 3.4–3.5 V filtered. No 9 V block. All supply
  numbers: 2.7.
- **Zigbee:** the hardware needs **nothing extra**. A coordinator alongside the device's own hotspot or alongside
  a BLE scan is **not supported (X)** by Espressif; Zigbee is only realistic on the home Wi-Fi.
- **Layers:** 4 layers (JLC04081H-3313), In1 solid GND, rules 0.15/0.15 mm, via 0.3/0.5 mm (chapter 11).
- **Routing:** complete with the own router: ERC 0, DRC 0 errors and 0 warnings, 0 unconnected, 0 GND islands,
  162 vias (rev B, 11.4).
- **Optional pads on the bottom:** player inputs J6, LED connector J7, test points, DNP footprints (11.7).

## 2. Module on the board

### 2.1 Footprint (verified)

Footprint `lcsc:WIFIM-SMD_ESP32-C6-MINI-1` (generated by `fetch_parts.py`): 53 pads on a 0.8 mm grid (0.8 × 0.4 mm),
pad 49 made of 9 sub-areas, courtyard 13.2 × 16.6 mm, 3D model of the C6. The pads are taken from the pad-identical
lcsc footprint of the ESP32-C3-MINI-1, because the EasyEDA footprint of the C6 is off the grid by up to 20 µm. GND pads 1, 2, 11, 14, 36–53;
3V3 = 3, EN = 8; NC = 4, 7, 21, 32–35. Antenna at the −y end of the footprint → board −x.

The KiCad footprint `RF_Module:ESP32-C6-MINI-1` is pad-identical (pad 49 shifted by max. 5 µm), but has a courtyard
of 13.5 × 16.9 mm **plus a keepout zone of 43.2 × 20.4 mm** (unusable for `antenna_keepout()`) and in KiCad 9 no
3D model.

**All ADC pins (IO0–IO6) are in the +y half.** USB (IO12/13) sits in the middle of the right side and thus points

small DRV8213 (chapter 3) sits at the −y corner of the module.

### 2.4 Strapping, USB, reset

- **IO8:** 10 k to 3V3 (R11). **IO9:** internal pull-up is sufficient, pad J3. **IO15:** strapping has no effect
  without eFuse, R22 (DNP) to GND. **IO4/IO5:** level during boot irrelevant (SDIO timing). **IO2** is not a
  strapping pin on the C6; R10 is the SCL pull-up (verified, analysis-wireless-controllers.md 3b.1).
- **USB:** IO12 = D−, IO13 = D+. ESD (U5) directly at J4 and **flow-through**: J4 → U5 pin 1/3 → under the
  package → pin 6/4 → module. D+/D− cross at J4 (order B7 A6 A7 B6). No stub to J1: **flashing only via
  J4**; J1-2/3 have no net.
- **ESD finding (verified, ST DS4260 Rev 5):** on the USBLC6-2SC6, pins 1/6 (I/O1) and 3/4 (I/O2) are connected internally.
  ST shows a branch to the ESD as "Unsuitable layout" (Fig. 7) and flow-through as "Optimized layout"
  (Fig. 18): D+in pin 1 → D+out pin 6, D−in pin 3 → D−out pin 4. Every stub adds L·di/dt to the
  clamping voltage (approx. 6 nH at 10 mm → +144 V at 8 kV). VBUS on pin 5, GND on pin 2; the 100 nF capacitor on VBUS
  recommended by ST is not fitted (D2 and the 47 µF buffers sit behind it). Second source UMW C2687116,
  same pinout.
- **EN:** R2 10 k / C12 1 µF. On the C6, download works automatically via USB-Serial-JTAG; BOOT remains on the
  pad as a fallback.

### 2.5 Wake-up via SENSE

**Circuit: R3 100 k OUT_A → GND, R4 10 k OUT_B → 3V3, R5 470 k SENSE → IO3.** "Lever closed" = HIGH.
R5 is only a series resistor into the high-impedance input (it does not change the levels below); 470 k limits the
current into the IO3 clamp diodes while OUT_A switches up to VSYS (6.6 V: approx. 6 µA; with 100 k it was 30 µA) and keeps
the level error from input leakage small (approx. 50 nA × 470 k ≈ 25 mV).

| State | SENSE | Wake-up |
|---|---|---|
| Lever open | 0 V (via 100 k) | – |
| Lever closed | 3.3 V × 100/110 ≈ **3.0 V** | ext1 wake-up ANY_HIGH on IO3 |
| Motor running | not meaningful, current via IPROPI | – |

Quiescent current with the lever closed approx. 30 µA. At VM = 5.5 V, OUT_B feeds approx. 0.2 mA back into the 3V3 rail;
the load absorbs this (not critical). **Special case:** if the lever stays closed when going to sleep, the firmware must
wake on LOW or check via timer (e.g. every 5 s). Check this case in the test setup ([test-hardware.md](test-hardware.md) 4.2).
Why not the inverse level: decision-history.md.

### 2.6 Antenna (finding L2 from analysis-wireless-controllers.md)

`antenna_keepout()`: rule areas "Antenna", "Antenna edge" and "Antenna end" **on all four layers**, with
copper fill and vias prohibited, **tracks allowed**. There is no GND under the antenna; the OUT_A track to BT1
runs on the bottom in the edge strip. The edge strip is at the **−y** edge (OUT_A runs from the DRV8213 at the
−y module corner to BT1 without crossing; electrically equivalent to +y, uncertain → RSSI test in both mounting orientations)
(analysis-wireless-controllers.md 2.3).

## 3. Motor control

**Decision: DRV8213DSGR (C22407186), GAINSEL to GND, R_IPROPI (R1) 1.24 k (1 %) to GND, followed by R8 1 k /
C14 100 nF to the ADC (IO1).** Do not put the capacitor directly on IPROPI, it would delay the current regulation.
Rationale: current sensing, current limiting and autosleep are built in; this saves a shunt, nSLEEP/nFAULT, 2 GPIOs and
approx. 30 mm² compared with a driver in HTSSOP-16. Rejected drivers (DRV8833, DRV8212, DRV8837): decision-history.md.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| VM | **1.65–11 V** (verified) |
| Rds(on) HS+LS | 240 mΩ typ. (GAINSEL = Low) (verified) |
| Current | 4 A peak, OCP approx. 4 A, RθJA 65.9 K/W (verified) |
| Current sensing | **IPROPI** 205 µA/A, ±6 % from 350 mA (verified) |

### 3.2 Design

- Runs from 3.0 V battery up to 5.5 V USB, brakes, reverses and is high-impedance in coast/autosleep (needed for
  lever detection; whether no output pull-down is active in sleep is uncertain → check on the prototype board).
  Current limit 2 A.
- **Stall detection:** IPROPI measures while driving **and** during the brake phase. Therefore PWM as **drive/brake**:
  forward IN1 = 1, IN2 = PWM with duty cycle (1 − d). This gives 255 mV/A at the ADC. Hardware stall detection
  exists only in the RTE package; the firmware evaluates current > threshold over a time x.
- **Heat:** 1 A → 0.24 W → +16 K; 2 A continuous (stalled) → approx. 1 W → +63 K (verified, calculated with RθJA). The
  firmware must switch off a stall after ≤ 0.5 s. 2 thermal vias (0.3 mm) in the EP.
- **PWM:** LEDC at 20 kHz with approx. 11-bit resolution is sufficient; MCPWM is not needed.
- **Inputs:** IN1/IN2 have internal 200 k pull-downs in the DRV8213. **During** reset, the internal ~45 k pull-ups
  of IO18/IO19 pull both inputs HIGH → brake (no drive, no start-up possible); after reset both are 0
  (coast) (verified, C6 datasheet Tab. 2-1/2-2). Decision open (F17): leave as is, or add external 3.3 k pull-downs for
  coast during reset as well. Measurement in reset, boot and deep sleep: [test-hardware.md](test-hardware.md) 4.4.
- The DRV8213 and motor operation are only tested on the prototype board; on the breadboard there is no
  motor driver, only pre-order checks (motor current on the lab power supply, lever network, levels) and LED mode
  ([test-hardware.md](test-hardware.md) 4, 5).

| Stack | `esp-zigbee-sdk`, precompiled libraries, ESP-IDF 5.5.x, C API | (verified) |
| Rust firmware | Since the firmware runs on ESP-IDF (std, esp-idf-sys), the SDK can be included as an ESP-IDF component and called via FFI | (uncertain, not tried) |
| Flash/RAM | Roughly +0.5–0.8 MB app, 50–100 KB RAM for a small coordinator. 8 MB is recommended for OTA | (uncertain) |
| Thread/Matter | same coexistence limits (Thread router + SoftAP = X). Matter over Wi-Fi needs no 802.15.4 | (verified / uncertain) |

**Assessment:** with the device's own hotspot (party mode, 8 phones), a Zigbee coordinator is **ruled out**, and
likewise alongside the BLE BTHome buttons. The only practical option would be "Louie on the home Wi-Fi (STA) + Zigbee". There, however,
it is simpler to connect the buttons via Home Assistant over Wi-Fi. **What is provided:** C6, **8 MB flash (H8)**, the
antenna keepout areas (2.6) and a good mounting location. Zigbee is **no reason for additional parts**
(operation: [idea-zigbee-operation.md](idea-zigbee-operation.md)).

**Without a sensor:** the pull-ups on A keep the bus in a defined state. At startup the firmware does an I²C scan (0x18/0x19)

(or a pull-up for a switch to GND). This has no effect if nothing is connected.

### 5.3 Board B in the second compartment

- **Feasible:** like today's charger board, B takes over the bridge. The bridge track is **in the motor circuit**
  (PWM potential) and must remain **galvanically isolated** on B from GND/3V3 of the B electronics. The cable to A
  (5 wires for the sensor, 6 with charging circuit: BAT_IN, GND, 3V3, SDA, SCL, INT) runs across the partition as it does today.
  I²C over approx. 10–15 cm at 100 kHz is not critical (uncertain).
- **Old Louie (1 × C):** B sits in the C sleeve above A (hardware-concept.md 6: 2 boards with approx. 10 mm spacing
  fit). The bridge stays unused.
- **Is a dedicated B worth it?** For the sensor alone **no**: a ready-made breakout (LIS3DH/LIS2DH12/MPU6050, approx.
  €1–5, often approx. 16–20 mm wide, uncertain) fits in the base or the C sleeve, but probably not in the AA compartment. Even
  simpler is a **spring switch** (e.g. SW-18010P) between INT and GND: it wakes on shaking, without any I²C.
  **A board B is only worth it as "B charger + sensor"**, i.e. as a further development of today's charger board v0.2.

### 5.4 Recommendation

**A** = C6 + DRV8213 + buck converter + SENSE + VBAT + IPROPI + USB-C stage + LED, everything else as pads. **Manufacture only A
first**, with the copper bridge or the charger board in the second compartment. Sensor on top assembly optional, otherwise breakout
or spring switch on J2. The **charger board v0.1** remains usable on J1 (BAT_IN). A board B only later, and
then only as v0.2 "charger + sensor".

## 11. 4 layers: stack-up, rules, generator, routing

**Decision 2026-11-03: 4 layers** (reasons and the rejected 2-layer variant: decision-history.md).
The board is in `hardware/main-board/louie-main.kicad_pcb` (project `louie-main.kicad_pro`, rule file
`louie-main.kicad_dru`, routing result `routing.json`); `just pcb`, `just route`, `just erc`, `just drc`,
`just images`, `just model`, `just render`, `just fab` work on it without parameters. Everything is generated by the
scripts, nothing in the GUI.

### 11.1 Layer stack-up

**JLC standard stack-up JLC04081H-3313** (0.8 mm, 4 layers, outer 1 oz, inner 0.5 oz) **(uncertain)**: values from
a stack-up file generated from the template of the JLC impedance calculator (Hive-RSI-Lab/hive-hwde
PR 533, 2026-11-01); the JLC calculator itself only delivers the values via JavaScript and was not read.
The sum of 0.7992 mm matches the nominal thickness. Confirm in the JLC order form ("Layer stackup")
before ordering.

| Layer | Material | Thickness | εr | Use (chosen variant A) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| L1 F.Cu | copper 1 oz (incl. plating) | 0.035 mm | – | components, short signals, GND fill |
| Prepreg | 3313 (1 ×) | 0.0994 mm | 4.1 | |
| L2 In1.Cu | copper 0.5 oz | 0.0152 mm | – | **solid GND**, no tracks (keepout area "GND layer") |
| Core | FR4 | 0.5 mm | 4.48 | |
| L3 In2.Cu | copper 0.5 oz | 0.0152 mm | – | signals (crossings, buses), GND fill in between |
| Prepreg | 3313 (1 ×) | 0.0994 mm | 4.1 | |
| L4 B.Cu | copper 1 oz | 0.035 mm | – | solder pads (J1/J2/J3/J5/BT), motor outputs, signals, GND fill |

The stack-up is stored as `(stackup …)` in the `.kicad_pcb` (`STACKUP`, `write_stackup()` in `gen_main_pcb.py`).

**Layer assignment (variant A):** Sig / **GND** / Sig+GND / Sig+GND, i.e. 3 signal layers. Other assignments
(3V3 plane on L3, GND twice, GND on the outside) were rejected (decision-history.md).

- **Routability:** crossings and long buses run on L3; L4 does not have to be a ground plane. A 3V3 plane on L3
  only helps 3V3 (10 pads) and takes away the layer for crossings; in a trial run with Freerouting it was not better.
- **RF ground:** L2 is 0.1 mm below the components; module, DRV8213 and USB have a continuous reference plane.
  Under the antenna there is no copper **on any of the four layers**: the keepout areas "Antenna", "Antenna edge" and
  "Antenna end" from `antenna_keepout()` apply to `AllCuMask` and are stored in the file with
  `(layers "F.Cu" "B.Cu" "In1.Cu" "In2.Cu")` (verified); the GND fills of all layers end before the antenna.
- **USB:** Full Speed (12 Mbit/s) needs no impedance control. 0.15 mm tracks over GND at 0.1 mm spacing
  give roughly 50 Ω single-ended (uncertain, rule of thumb); the pair is pre-routed (ESD flow-through, 2.4).
- **Motor current:** the 0.4/0.5 mm main paths (OUT_A/OUT_B, VSYS, VBUS, BAT_IN) are pre-routed; the return currents
  flow through the solid L2.

### 11.9 Bottleneck at the right module pins (status 2026-11-09)

### 11.10 Review findings accepted for this revision (status 2026-11-10)

From the datasheet and wiring review (wiring-review.md); checked whether placement can improve them:
- **C12 (EN capacitor) approx. 12 mm from EN pad 8:** pad 8 is on the module edge at the board edge; the best free top
  spot would be 9.6 mm, the former place (approx. 7 mm) now holds the VBAT divider. EN has a continuous GND reference on

- **No 3V3 capacitor at module pad 3:** below it is the board edge, to the left the antenna keepout, above it the
